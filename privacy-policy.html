This policy explains how we may collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit this website, contact us, submit the website enquiry form, or discuss our services with us.

It does not replace the specific contractual, security, confidentiality, or data-processing terms that may apply to a client engagement. Those terms should be agreed in writing before any assessment or investigation begins.

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive your name, job title, organization, email address, telephone number, message content, requested service, and information you choose to provide about your security requirements.

We may also receive limited technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location, referring page, and interaction data from hosting, security, analytics, or form-service providers. Configure analytics and cookies separately if they are introduced to the website.

4. How we use information

We may use information to respond to enquiries, schedule discussions, prepare proposals, provide requested services, administer client relationships, maintain records, protect the website, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, and improve the clarity and security of our services.

We do not use information supplied through the website to access, test, or investigate a system. Security testing only begins after written authorization, agreed scope, and applicable engagement documentation are in place.

5. Legal bases and permissions

Where applicable, we rely on consent, steps taken at your request before entering a contract, performance of a contract, legitimate interests, and compliance with legal obligations. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it by contacting us, although this does not affect processing already completed lawfully.

The website enquiry form currently uses FormSubmit to transmit form submissions to iForcehk@consultant.com. FormSubmit is an independent third-party provider. Review its current privacy documentation and configure the service in accordance with your legal and operational requirements before accepting live enquiries.

We may share information with service providers who help us host, secure, communicate through, or operate the website, with professional advisers, with law-enforcement or regulatory authorities where required, or with another party where you have instructed or authorized us to do so. We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, to maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or comply with legal obligations. Client engagement records are governed by the applicable contract and security requirements.

9. International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom or your home jurisdiction. Where required, we will seek to use an appropriate legal transfer mechanism and apply suitable safeguards. Confirm the locations and transfer terms of each provider before launch.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information, and to complain to a data-protection regulator. To exercise a right or ask a privacy question, contact us using the details above. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

This website is intended for business visitors and is not directed to children. Please do not submit personal information through the site if you are under the age required by applicable law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

13. Contact
