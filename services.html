Core capabilities

Security services built for clarity and action.

Ifoce Hackers Intelligence combines authorized offensive testing with defensive investigation, threat modeling, reporting, and verified remediation.

Assess the real risk

A complete view of your digital environment.

We identify vulnerabilities, explain their business impact, and help you prioritize the improvements that matter most.

Vulnerability Assessment

A vulnerability assessment provides a structured review of your digital environment. We examine websites, servers, networks, applications, endpoints, and cloud systems to identify weaknesses and rank them according to risk.

Penetration Testing

Penetration testing goes further by safely simulating realistic attack scenarios. Within the agreed scope, our team verifies whether vulnerabilities can be exploited, evaluates their potential impact, and documents the path an attacker could take.

Web Applications, Networks, and Endpoints

Modern attacks can begin almost anywhere, which is why we assess the systems and access points most relevant to your organization.

  • Web applications and APIs
  • Networks and infrastructure
  • Endpoints and devices
  • Cloud systems and configurations

Authorized Social Engineering

With explicit authorization and carefully agreed rules of engagement, we can conduct social engineering tests—like simulated phishing—to see if employees can spot manipulation attempts. This is done sensitively, focusing on training rather than blame.

Configuration Reviews

Security configuration reviews hunt down risky settings and excessive user privileges that no firewall would catch. We look for insecure settings, weak access controls, and unnecessary exposure.

Digital Forensics

If a security incident occurs, digital forensics step in to investigate what happened, preserve evidence, and understand the breach’s scope.

Threat Modeling

Threat modeling anticipates possible attack paths before anything goes wrong. We map critical assets, trust boundaries, and controls so teams can break attack paths before a breach occurs.

Reporting and Retesting

Every engagement concludes with detailed reports explaining findings in plain language, recommendations for fixes, and retesting to confirm vulnerabilities are truly resolved.
